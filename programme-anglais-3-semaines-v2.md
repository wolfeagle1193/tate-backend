# Programme Anglais – Taté 🏆
## Format : Théorie (25min) → Débat/Pratique (40min) → Correction (25min)
## Durée : 3 semaines | 3 séances/semaine | 1h30/séance | 30 000 FCFA

---

## 📅 SEMAINE 1 : Les bases pour communiquer

### Séance 1
▸ **PARTIE 1 (25min) :** Présentation + Verbe TO BE (I am, you are...)
▸ **DÉBAT (40min) :** *"Introduce yourself – Tell me about your job and background"*
▸ **CORRECTION (25min) :** Erreurs + vocabulaire professionnel

### Séance 2
▸ **PARTIE 1 (25min) :** Present Simple + DO/DOES (questions)
▸ **DÉBAT (40min) :** *"Describe your daily routine – What do you do at work?"*
▸ **CORRECTION (25min) :** Prononciation + nouveaux mots

### Séance 3
▸ **PARTIE 1 (25min) :** Present Continuous + CAN / MAY
▸ **DÉBAT (40min) :** *"What are you doing to improve your career?"*
▸ **CORRECTION (25min) :** Structure des phrases + video recap

## 📅 SEMAINE 2 : Passé, futur et nuances

### Séance 4
▸ **PARTIE 1 (25min) :** Simple Past (réguliers/irréguliers) + DID
▸ **DÉBAT (40min) :** *"Tell me about your education and career journey"*
▸ **CORRECTION (25min) :** Erreurs de conjugaison + nouveau vocabulaire

### Séance 5
▸ **PARTIE 1 (25min) :** Future (WILL/GOING TO) + MUST / HAVE TO
▸ **DÉBAT (40min) :** *"What are your goals for the next 5 years?"*
▸ **CORRECTION (25min) :** Débat + vidéo d'entraînement

### Séance 6
▸ **PARTIE 1 (25min) :** Present Perfect + MAY / MIGHT
▸ **DÉBAT (40min) :** *"Have you ever been abroad? What did you learn?"*
▸ **CORRECTION (25min) :** Simulation : situation professionnelle

## 📚 RÉFÉRENCES EXPRESS

### Temps à maîtriser :
1. **Present Simple** → habitudes (I work)
2. **Present Continuous** → maintenant (I am working)
3. **Simple Past** → passé fini (I worked / I went)
4. **Present Perfect** → expérience (I have worked)
5. **Future** → projets (I will work / I am going to work)

### Auxiliaires modaux :
- **CAN** = capacité → *I can drive*
- **MAY** = permission/possibilité → *May I come in?*
- **MUST** = obligation → *You must study*
- **WILL** = futur → *I will call you*

### Question words :
- **WHAT** → quoi / **WHERE** → où / **WHEN** → quand
- **WHY** → pourquoi / **HOW** → comment / **WHO** → qui

### Débats préparés :
1. Your daily routine
2. Your career goals
3. Is experience > education ?
4. Will AI replace engineers ?
5. What would you change in your life ?
